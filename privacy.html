Privacy Policy

Growth Project Uploader · Effective November 4, 2026

Growth Project Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by a single creator to publish videos to, and read analytics from, their own YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, captions, and playlists on the creator's own channel, and read that channel's analytics.
  • TikTok: Login Kit (basic profile: user.info.basic) to identify the creator's own account, and the Content Posting API (video.upload) to send the creator's own videos to the TikTok app inbox as drafts. The creator reviews and posts each draft manually in the TikTok app. The app stores only the access/refresh tokens and the account's open ID, locally.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the creator's own Instagram account and read its insights.

The app does not access any other person's account or data, and it does not collect information from viewers.

How data is used and stored

  • Access is used only to publish the creator's own approved content and to analyze that content's performance.
  • Authorization tokens and analytics data are stored locally on the creator's own computer. They are not stored on any third-party server operated by the app.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Instagram data is handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: Google security settings → Third-party apps & services
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites

When access is revoked, locally stored tokens stop working and are deleted, along with stored analytics, on request.
