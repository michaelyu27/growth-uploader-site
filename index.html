Growth Project Uploader

A private publishing tool for a single creator's own channels.

Growth Project Uploader is an internal tool used by one creator to publish original, self-produced story videos to their own YouTube channel, TikTok account, and Instagram account, and to read the performance analytics of those same accounts.

The tool is not offered to the public. It has no other users, does not access anyone else's accounts, and does not sell or share data.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules the creator's own finished videos, thumbnails, and captions.
  • Reads the creator's own channel analytics to improve future videos.
  • Acts only after the creator approves each upload.
